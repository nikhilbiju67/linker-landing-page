# Linkker blog formats

The picker rotates these so the blog never reads as one template repeated.
Every format renders the same way: a short intro, a "Quick picks" box (built from
each item's bestFor), one card per site, then the outro.

## top-list
Length 900-1900 words. Items 8-12.
"The N best free X". The intro says in two sentences who the list is for and what
made a site qualify. Order items by how many readers each one suits, the safest
all-rounder first. Outro: one H2 on how to choose between them, then an H2
"Quick answers" with 3 short Q&As in the searcher's words.
---

## hidden-gems
Length 700-1500 words. Items 7-12.
Lesser-known websites that do something surprisingly useful or delightful. Each
item says the exact moment you would reach for it. Outro: one H2 with a short
tip on keeping track of sites like these, then "Quick answers" with 2-3 Q&As.
---

## how-to-tools
Length 700-1400 words. Items 5-8.
"How to do X for free": the intro answers the how in 2-3 sentences. Items are the
sites that do it. Outro: an H2 with numbered steps for the most common case
using the first site, then "Quick answers" with 2-3 Q&As.
---

## alternatives
Length 800-1600 words. Items 6-10.
"Free alternatives to <paid tool>". The intro names what people miss from the
paid tool and what free tools can and cannot replace. Each item says what it
replaces well and where it falls short. Outro: a markdown table comparing the
items on 3 short columns (best for, works in, catch), then "Quick answers".
---

## starter-kit
Length 800-1600 words. Items 8-12.
Sites for a group with a shared job (students, freelancers, designers, new
developers). Group order by the job of the week, not by fame. Outro: an H2 with a
short "set it up in 10 minutes" routine, then "Quick answers".
---

## linkker-guide
Length 700-1300 words. Items 4-8.
Saving, organizing and finding links: read-later apps, bookmark managers,
moving links between phone and computer. Linkker may be one honest item among
others, never first by default. Outro: an H2 with a practical routine, then
"Quick answers".
---
