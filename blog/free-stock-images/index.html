The 9 best free stock images websites, with every catch explained

By the Linkker team · 12 November 2026 · 7 min read

These free stock images websites suit bloggers, students, designers, and small businesses that need visuals for websites, presentations, or social posts.

Each offers usable image downloads without a trial timer, and made the list only after checking its official site and licensing terms, with the broadest everyday options first.

Quick picks

  1. Pexels: Everyday photos and matching video footage
  2. Pixabay: Projects needing several types of visual assets
  3. Burst: Small business websites and store banners
  4. Kaboompics: Matching lifestyle, interior, and brand visuals
  5. Wikimedia Commons: Educational images and specific factual subjects
  6. ISO Republic: Extra photo and video options under CC0
  7. NegativeSpace: Browsing familiar photo subjects by category
  8. Stockvault: Textures, backgrounds, and general stock photos
  9. Freerange Stock: Business websites, presentations, and promotional graphics
  10. Life of Pix: Photo-led websites, blog headers, and design projects
  11. rawpixel: Projects that need both photos and graphic elements
  12. Foodiesfeed: Food blogs, restaurant websites, and culinary content
  1. 1

    Pexels

    pexels.com
    Free

    Find free stock photos and videos for everyday creative projects.

    Start with Pexels when you need a photo for a blog, presentation, or social post and want a straightforward download. Having photos and videos together also helps when one project needs both a cover image and background footage.

    The Pexels license permits free use and editing without required attribution. The practical limit is how you use the subject: a recognizable person or brand cannot be presented as endorsing your business. You also cannot sell an unaltered photo as a print or redistribute it through another stock library.

    Best for
    Everyday photos and matching video footage
    The catch
    You cannot sell unaltered copies, redistribute files as stock, or imply endorsement by people or brands pictured.
    Visit pexels.com ↗
  2. 2

    Pixabay

    pixabay.com
    Free

    Search free photos, illustrations, vectors, and video in one place.

    Use Pixabay when a photograph is only one possible answer. Its separate media categories let you look for an illustration, vector, or clip instead, which is helpful for explaining an abstract topic or finding a background.

    The official FAQ confirms that its media is free for personal and commercial projects, with credit appreciated rather than required. That permission does not automatically cover recognizable brands, people, or other protected elements inside an image. For business graphics, inspect the actual file rather than treating the download button as clearance for everything pictured.

    Best for
    Projects needing several types of visual assets
    The catch
    Free downloads do not automatically clear trademarks, privacy rights, or publicity rights associated with the subject.
    Visit pixabay.com ↗
  3. 3

    Burst

    shopify.com
    Free

    Find free business and lifestyle photos for stores, websites, and marketing.

    Burst is Shopify’s photo library, with collections organized around products, retail, work, and everyday life. That focus helps a small business get from a vague request such as “something for our shop banner” to a usable set of candidates.

    Shopify’s guidance confirms free use and editing without required attribution. You can use the images beyond a Shopify store. Check the license attached to your photo: Burst uses both CC0 and its own license, and the latter prohibits selling unaltered files or compiling a competing photo service.

    Best for
    Small business websites and store banners
    The catch
    Photos carry different license labels; Burst’s own license restricts unaltered resale and competing photo libraries.
    Visit shopify.com ↗
  4. 4

    Kaboompics

    kaboompics.com
    Free

    Find coordinated lifestyle photos that work together across a design.

    Kaboompics is particularly useful when you need several images with matching light, colors, and styling. Its gallery supports browsing by color and orientation, exploring complete photoshoots, and selecting multiple images for batch download.

    That makes it a practical choice for a website refresh or a series of social posts. Under the Standard License, personal and commercial use is free without required credit. Check every file’s label, though: Editorial Use Only images cannot go into ads, packaging, or brand marketing. Using the images for AI training or image generation is also prohibited.

    Best for
    Matching lifestyle, interior, and brand visuals
    The catch
    Some files are Editorial Use Only; AI training, image generation, and standalone image resale are prohibited.
    Visit kaboompics.com ↗
  5. 5

    Wikimedia Commons

    commons.wikimedia.org
    Free

    Find freely reusable images of specific places, objects, historical subjects, and scientific topics.

    Choose Wikimedia Commons when accuracy matters more than a polished stock-photo look. It is useful for a particular landmark, historical figure, species, or technical subject that a general lifestyle library may not cover well.

    The tradeoff is extra reading. Its reuse guide explains that files carry individual licenses or public-domain information. Some need an author credit and license link; some also require shared adaptations to use the same license. Download from the file page and keep its attribution details alongside the image so they survive the trip into your project.

    Best for
    Educational images and specific factual subjects
    The catch
    Licenses vary by file; attribution, license links, and share-alike requirements may apply.
    Visit commons.wikimedia.org ↗
  6. 6

    ISO Republic

    isorepublic.com
    Free with ads

    Browse free stock photos and videos released under CC0.

    ISO Republic gives you another source for office scenes, nature, technology, textures, and everyday objects when the first few libraries do not produce the right fit. Its homepage lets you separate photos from videos before searching.

    The license page confirms free personal and commercial downloads without required attribution. It also makes a useful disclosure: photographs containing people may not have model releases. For an advertisement built around someone’s face, that is a meaningful limitation. Sponsored Shutterstock links are separate from the site’s own free downloads.

    Best for
    Extra photo and video options under CC0
    The catch
    People pictured may lack model releases, and sponsored links lead outside the free library.
    Visit isorepublic.com ↗
  7. 7

    NegativeSpace

    negativespace.co
    Free

    Browse free photographs by subject under a single CC0 license.

    NegativeSpace works well as an additional browsing source for architecture, landscapes, transport, business, and other familiar subjects. Its category list is useful when you would rather scan a topic than keep rewriting a search query.

    The site’s license places its photographs under CC0, allowing free personal and commercial use without required attribution. The catch is that the site leaves checking the validity and suitability of an image to you. Use it for another set of candidates, and inspect recognizable subjects before putting a photograph into promotional material.

    Best for
    Browsing familiar photo subjects by category
    The catch
    The site makes you responsible for checking whether a particular photo is suitable for your intended use.
    Visit negativespace.co ↗
  8. 8

    Stockvault

    stockvault.net
    Free

    Find free photos, textures, and illustrations without creating an account.

    Stockvault is useful for backgrounds, surface textures, and everyday subjects. Downloads are free, but permissions vary by image. Its official FAQ explains the available licenses and confirms that downloading does not require registration.

    Best for
    Textures, backgrounds, and general stock photos
    The catch
    Some images allow only noncommercial use. Check each image's license before using it for a business.
    Visit stockvault.net ↗
  9. 9

    Freerange Stock

    freerangestock.com
    Free

    Download free stock photos and illustrations for websites, presentations, and marketing.

    Freerange offers images for personal and commercial projects without requiring attribution. Its license covers common design uses, including websites and promotional materials.

    Best for
    Business websites, presentations, and promotional graphics
    The catch
    Its standard license prohibits selling prints or putting images on merchandise for sale. Images with brands or logos are restricted to editorial use.
    Visit freerangestock.com ↗
  10. 10

    Life of Pix

    lifeofpix.com
    Free

    Browse free high-resolution photography for personal and commercial projects.

    Life of Pix provides photographs you can download, edit, and use without mandatory credit. Its license permits commercial projects while placing limits on resale and redistribution.

    Best for
    Photo-led websites, blog headers, and design projects
    The catch
    You cannot resell unchanged photos or redistribute images in bulk without permission. The site requires JavaScript to browse.
    Visit lifeofpix.com ↗
  11. 11

    rawpixel

    rawpixel.com
    Free plan

    Find free stock photos alongside illustrations, backgrounds, and public-domain artwork.

    rawpixel's free collection combines photography with other useful design assets. Its ongoing free plan supports personal and business use, with separate unlimited downloads from the public-domain collection.

    Best for
    Projects that need both photos and graphic elements
    The catch
    An account is required, regular free assets have a daily download cap, and premium images are excluded. Stay within the free or public-domain collections.
    Visit rawpixel.com ↗
  12. 12

    Foodiesfeed

    foodiesfeed.com
    Free

    Download free food and drink images for blogs, menus, and social posts.

    Foodiesfeed focuses on meals, ingredients, and drinks. Its terms allow free personal and commercial downloads with an optional account. The linked Japanese homepage is reachable and offers category browsing.

    Best for
    Food blogs, restaurant websites, and culinary content
    The catch
    The library includes AI-generated images, so check individual entries if you need actual photography. The linked homepage is in Japanese.
    Visit foodiesfeed.com ↗

How to choose between these free stock images websites

For a routine blog post or presentation, start with Pexels, then try Unsplash. Move to Pixabay when an illustration or vector would explain the idea better than a photograph. Burst suits business themes, while Kaboompics helps when several images need to look like they belong together.

For a named place, historical subject, or scientific topic, use Wikimedia Commons and budget a little time for attribution. ISO Republic and NegativeSpace are useful extra sources when your first searches stall. Save Gratisography for projects that can carry its playful style.

Before downloading, check three things: the image’s license, whether its subjects fit your intended use, and whether the composition survives your crop. A beautiful horizontal photo may become useless in a narrow mobile card. Keep the source page and license details with the finished asset, especially when a client or colleague will publish it later.

For websites, resize the image to the dimensions you actually need before uploading. If the resulting file is still too heavy, these free tools to reduce image size cover the next step.

Quick answers

Where can I get free stock images no watermark?

Pexels and the free Unsplash library are good starting points. Download the actual image rather than saving a preview, and check its license before publishing. No watermark does not mean there are no usage restrictions.

Which free stock video websites offer free stock video download?

Pexels, Pixabay, and ISO Republic offer free stock videos alongside images. Choose the video category before searching, and use the clip’s own download page to check the applicable terms.

Are free stock photos free for commercial use?

Many are, including photos covered by the Pexels and free Unsplash licenses. Commercial permission still comes with conditions: recognizable people, logos, and artwork may need additional clearance. Wikimedia Commons requires a file-by-file license check, and Kaboompics marks some images Editorial Use Only.
