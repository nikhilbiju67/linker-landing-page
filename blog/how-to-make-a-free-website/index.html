8 tools for how to make a free website, with every catch explained

By the Linkker team · 3 November 2026 · 6 min read

To make a free website for a portfolio, club, or small project, choose a builder below, customize a template, and publish on its included web address. Start with Google Sites for a straightforward site, Carrd for one page, or WordPress.com for a blog.

Quick picks

  1. Google Sites: A straightforward first website
  2. Carrd: A personal page or compact portfolio
  3. Wix: Visual editing and an AI-assisted start
  4. WordPress.com: Blogs and regularly updated writing
  5. Canva: Visual portfolios and event pages
  6. Webflow: Learning visual web design
  7. GitHub Pages: Personal coding projects and documentation
  8. Cloudflare Pages: Hosting a custom static website
  1. 1

    Google Sites

    workspace.google.com
    Free

    Build a simple website with text, images, pages, and embedded Google documents.

    Google Sites suits a club homepage, class project, resource directory, or basic portfolio. Arrange content in ready-made layouts rather than designing every element yourself. Its useful extra is embedding Google Forms, calendars, and documents, so a site can hold more than a few paragraphs and pictures.

    Use a personal Google account to get started. Publishing and viewer permissions are separate: check that your published site is public if you want anyone to visit. Google's publishing instructions explain that setting.

    Best for
    A straightforward first website
    The catch
    Requires a Google account. Design controls are limited, and it lacks a built-in store or full blogging system.

    Free with ads

    WordPress.com is the practical pick when publishing articles is the main job. Pages hold permanent information such as your bio; posts handle updates, essays, and tutorials. That structure is more useful for an ongoing blog than stretching a landing-page builder into one.

    The free plan has no expiration and includes hosting, themes, and the block editor. Choose a theme close to your intended layout, then focus on readable content. This is the hosted service, so you do not need to arrange a separate server.

How to make a free website with Google Sites

  1. Start with your content. Write a short introduction, gather a few images, and decide what visitors should do next. For a basic portfolio, prepare Home, Work, and Contact pages.
  2. Open Google Sites on a computer. Sign in with a personal Google account, then choose a blank site or a template. Give the site a recognizable name.
  3. Build the homepage. Add a clear title, a short explanation, and one useful action, such as viewing your projects or opening a contact form. Replace all template placeholders.
  4. Add supporting pages. Use the Pages panel to create your navigation. Insert images and documents where they help. Check sharing permissions on embedded files so visitors can actually open them.
  5. Preview different screen sizes. Check headings, image crops, navigation, and buttons. If a large PDF is part of your portfolio, these free tools to compress PDF files can help reduce its size.
  6. Publish and check access. Click Publish, choose an available address, and confirm publication. In sharing settings, set the published site to Public if anyone should be able to view it. Open the published link while signed out and check every important link. Google's publishing guide covers the access controls.

Quick answers

How to make a free website on Google?

Use Google Sites with a personal Google account, add your content, and publish to its included Google address. You do not need a Workspace subscription for a basic personal site. Publishing a website does not guarantee that it will appear in Google search results.

How to make a free website with AI?

Wix lets you start with an AI-generated site and publish using its free website plan. Describe the site's purpose and audience, then review every generated claim, image, and contact detail. The published site keeps Wix branding and a Wix address; AI use has limits. See Wix's free-plan explanation.

Can I make a free website with my own domain?

Free hosting and a free domain are separate things. Most visual builders here reserve custom-domain connections for paid plans. GitHub Pages and Cloudflare Pages support domains you already own, but registering and renewing that domain is a separate expense. Use the included platform address to keep the whole setup free.
