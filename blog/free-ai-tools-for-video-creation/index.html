The 8 best free AI tools for video creation

By the Linkker team · 16 October 2026 · 7 min read

These free AI tools for video creation suit beginners, students, and creators who want to edit footage, add captions, or make a video from a script.

Each qualifies through an ongoing free plan or free local software that handles the job described, with the limits explained before you commit to an edit.

Quick picks

  1. Microsoft Clipchamp: Most beginners editing existing footage
  2. Adobe Express: Captioning short explainers and social clips
  3. Descript: Interviews and talking-head videos
  4. Shotcut: Desktop editing without cloud usage caps
  5. HeyGen: Occasional scripted presenter videos
  6. Vidnoz AI: Short template-based avatar presentations
  7. ComfyUI: Technical users generating footage locally
  8. Canva: Captioning social clips and simple explainers
  9. Kdenlive: Desktop editing with automatic subtitles
  10. Fliki: Short narrated explainers made from text
  11. VEED: Adding readable captions to short social videos
  12. Kapwing: Occasional browser-based editing and captioning
  1. 1

    Microsoft Clipchamp

    clipchamp.com
    Free plan

    Edit your footage, generate captions, and add AI narration in a browser.

    Clipchamp is the safest starting point for everyday videos: tutorials, narrated slides, simple promos, and clips assembled from your own recordings. Its useful AI features include automatic captions, text-to-speech, silence removal, and auto compose, which arranges supplied photos and footage.

    The distinction matters: auto compose builds with your media rather than inventing moving scenes from a prompt. You can export free projects in 1080p without a watermark when using included assets. Choose it when you want AI help inside a conventional editor. Microsoft's AI feature guide explains the included tools.

    Best for
    Most beginners editing existing footage
    The catch
    Sign-in is required; premium stock assets and higher-resolution exports are outside the free offering.
    Visit clipchamp.com ↗
  2. 2

    Adobe Express

    adobe.com
    Free plan

    Generate readable captions for a short video and keep editing the result.

    Adobe Express is a practical pick when your video already exists and needs captions, titles, music, or a cleaner presentation. Its Caption Video tool transcribes speech, lets you correct the wording, and downloads the captioned result as an MP4.

    The standout is the short path from transcription to a finished clip: you can download immediately or continue editing in Express. Use it for explainers, class presentations, and spoken social posts. The caption tool is free; its technical requirements impose clip-length, upload-size, and language restrictions.

    Best for
    Captioning short explainers and social clips
    The catch
    Caption uploads have length and file-size limits, supported languages are restricted, and premium assets require an will
    Visit adobe.com ↗
  3. 3

    Descript

    descript.com
    Free plan

    Edit spoken video by cutting and rearranging its transcript.

    Descript makes sense for interviews, tutorials, and videos where the speaker carries the story. Import a recording, generate a transcript, then remove or rearrange text to change the corresponding footage. That approach makes a first cut easier to follow than a timeline full of tiny audio segments.

    Its ongoing free plan includes a monthly media allowance and AI credits, with watermark-free export at a limited resolution. Use those allowances for short projects rather than a backlog of interviews. Descript's editor page separates the recurring free features from trial access to AI speech.

    Best for
    Interviews and talking-head videos
    The catch
    Monthly media and AI allowances are limited, free exports have a resolution cap, and some advanced features have trial.
    Visit descript.com ↗
  4. 4 S

    Shotcut

    shotcut.org
    Free & open source

    Edit video locally and generate subtitles with Whisper-based speech recognition.

    Shotcut suits people who want a full desktop editor without cloud accounts or recurring transcription credits. Its AI contribution is specific: speech-to-text generates a subtitle track from your video's audio. You still handle the edit, titles, and pacing yourself.

    The useful detail is model choice. You can use the included speech model or download a larger one that may improve recognition while taking longer to process. Pick Shotcut for repeated captioning and conventional editing on your own computer. The official site provides the free editor, and its speech-to-text guide explains the workflow.

    Best for
    Desktop editing without cloud usage caps
    The catch
    Installation is required, transcription speed depends on your computer, and generated subtitles need proofreading.
    Visit shotcut.org ↗
  5. 5

    HeyGen

    heygen.com
    Free plan

    Turn a written script into a video delivered by an AI presenter.

    HeyGen is useful when you need a presenter on screen but do not want to record yourself. Write a short script, choose an available avatar and voice, and generate a talking presentation for an introduction, lesson, or product explanation.

    Its free plan has a recurring monthly video allowance, so basic creation is not tied to a countdown trial. Keep the script brief and preview pronunciation carefully, especially for names and technical terms. Choose HeyGen for occasional presenter videos. Its plan comparison distinguishes ongoing free generation from limited trials of premium avatar and translation features.

    Best for
    Occasional scripted presenter videos
    The catch
    Sign-up, short-video and monthly creation limits apply; watermark removal and expanded premium features require an up.
    Visit heygen.com ↗
  6. 6

    Vidnoz AI

    vidnoz.com
    Free plan

    Create short avatar-led videos using scripts, voices, and presentation templates.

    Vidnoz AI is another option for turning a script into a presenter video, particularly when a ready-made layout helps you start. Combine an avatar, text, and supporting visuals for a short announcement, training explanation, or introduction.

    Its distinguishing free-plan detail is a daily creation allowance rather than only a monthly allocation. That can suit small, occasional projects spread across several days. Expect branded, lower-resolution output and a restricted selection of features. The official plan comparison confirms the ongoing free tier, daily allowance, export limits, and the separation between basic and premium tools.

    Best for
    Short template-based avatar presentations
    The catch
    Free videos carry branding, daily generation and clip-length caps apply, and export resolution is limited.
    Visit vidnoz.com ↗
  7. 7

    ComfyUI

    comfy.org
    Free & open source

    Generate video locally with downloadable models and configurable workflows.

    ComfyUI is the technical option for creating new moving footage from text or images. Run it locally with a supported downloadable video model, such as Wan, and connect the generation steps through a visual workflow.

    The standout is control: you can reuse a workflow and adjust its model, prompt, and generation settings. Local software avoids a service's daily credit cap, but setup and hardware are substantial requirements. Choose it only if you are comfortable downloading models and troubleshooting. Comfy explains its free local core, while the Wan workflow documentation covers text-to-video and image-to-video generation.

    Best for
    Technical users generating footage locally
    The catch
    Requires installation, large model downloads, compatible hardware, and setup time; cloud execution and partner models .
    Visit comfy.org ↗
  8. 8 C

    Canva

    canva.com
    Free plan

    Add automatic captions to your videos, then style them in the same editor.

    Upload a clip, generate captions from its speech, and correct any words it misses. You can adjust the text's appearance, trim scenes, and download the finished video as an MP4. Canva's official caption tool page confirms that automatic captioning is free.

    Best for
    Captioning social clips and simple explainers
    The catch
    An account is required. Some templates, stock assets, and other AI video features require a paid plan.
    Visit canva.com ↗
  9. 9

    Kdenlive

    kdenlive.org
    Free & open source

    Edit videos on your computer and generate subtitles with speech recognition.

    Kdenlive combines a multitrack video editor with speech recognition that can turn dialogue into subtitles. You can cut footage, mix audio, and correct the captions within one project. The editor is free and open source, and its speech-to-text documentation explains the supported engines.

    Best for
    Desktop editing with automatic subtitles
    The catch
    You must install the editor and configure speech recognition models. Transcription speed depends on your computer, and captions need proofreading.
    Visit kdenlive.org ↗
  10. 10

    Fliki

    fliki.ai
    Free plan

    Turn a short script into a narrated video using stock visuals and AI voices.

    Fliki builds videos from scripts or ideas, pairing narration with images, stock clips, and music. It suits a short explainer when you do not have footage to upload. Its official plan comparison confirms a continuing free plan with monthly credits.

    Best for
    Short narrated explainers made from text
    The catch
    Sign-up is required. Free videos carry a watermark, with limited monthly credits and export quality. Commercial rights, AI avatars, and generated video clips require a paid plan.
    Visit fliki.ai ↗
  11. 11

    VEED

    veed.io
    Free plan

    Generate captions for short videos and burn them into the finished clip.

    Upload your footage, let speech recognition create captions, then fix the wording and adjust the style. Exporting with captions embedded makes the text visible wherever you share the video. VEED's official subtitle page confirms free automatic captioning for short clips with monthly limits.

    Best for
    Adding readable captions to short social videos
    The catch
    Free exports have a watermark and quality limits. Automatic captions have a monthly allowance, and downloading separate subtitle files requires an upgrade.
    Visit veed.io ↗
  12. 12 K

    Kapwing

    kapwing.com
    Free plan

    Caption and edit a short video in your browser.

    Kapwing puts automatic subtitles, trimming, text overlays, and resizing in one workspace. It is useful for finishing an existing recording before sharing it. Its official free plan includes a limited allowance for automatic subtitles and text-to-speech, plus watermarked video exports.

    Best for
    Occasional browser-based editing and captioning
    The catch
    An account is required. Free exports carry a watermark and have resolution and length limits. AI tools have a small credit allowance.
    Visit kapwing.com ↗

How to choose between free AI video tools

Start with the material you already have. For recordings, screenshots, and photos, Clipchamp offers a straightforward route to an edited video with narration and captions. Adobe Express fits a quick captioning job. Descript is especially useful when removing spoken sentences is the main editing task.

Choose Shotcut when you want desktop editing and local transcription without a monthly service allowance. CapCut fits social editing, provided the tools you need are included in your region and version. For a scripted presenter, compare HeyGen and Vidnoz's free restrictions before writing a long script.

Creating entirely new footage is a different job. ComfyUI can do it locally, but the hardware requirement makes it a poor shortcut for someone who just needs a finished clip today. An editor assembling existing images can be the more practical choice.

Before committing to any project, export a short sample with the assets and features you intend to use. Check branding, resolution, caption placement, and whether the downloaded file plays correctly. That small check can reveal a restriction before you spend an afternoon editing.

Quick answers

What is a good free AI video maker?

Clipchamp is a sensible first choice for making videos from your own media: it includes AI captions, narration, and automatic assembly. For an AI presenter reading a script, HeyGen and Vidnoz offer ongoing free plans with creation limits. “Free AI video” rarely means every generation feature is unlimited.

Which free video editor app works on Mac?

Shotcut is a free video editor for Mac with local speech-to-text subtitles. Clipchamp also works on Mac through Chrome or Edge. Choose Shotcut for a desktop workflow and Clipchamp for browser editing; neither requires you to generate new footage to benefit from AI.

Can I get free image to video AI?

Yes, through local ComfyUI workflows with a compatible downloadable model. You need suitable hardware, storage, and time to set it up. Adding pans and zooms to a still image in an editor is easier, but it produces a different result from generating motion within the image.
