The 8 best free AI clip maker tools, with every catch explained

By the Linkker team · 17 November 2026 · 6 min read

These best free AI clip maker picks help podcasters, educators, and creators turn existing videos into Shorts, Reels, and other short clips.

Each qualifies through free clipping or AI-assisted editing you can actually export without a trial timer, with the watermark, usage limits, and extra work explained below.

Quick picks

  1. Vizard: Most creators who want editable AI clips
  2. OpusClip: Automatic clip suggestions with minimal setup
  3. Adobe Express: Clips that also need text and graphics
  4. 2short.ai: Spoken YouTube videos without export branding
  5. reap: Occasional captioned interview and lesson excerpts
  6. Kapwing: Prompt-directed clips with follow-up editing
  7. Microsoft Clipchamp: Visual highlight reels and montages
  8. FunClip: Technical users who want local transcript-based clipping

How to choose the right free AI clip maker

Start with Vizard if you want the AI to suggest moments and still need control over the final cut. OpusClip suits a faster download workflow, provided you can accept its lack of free editing. Adobe Express and Kapwing make more sense when the excerpt needs graphics, text, or further layout work.

For a captioned video already on your YouTube channel, 2short.ai is a focused option. reap offers another recurring clipping allowance if watermarked 720p output meets your needs. Clipchamp fits visual recaps assembled from footage, while FunClip suits readers comfortable installing local software.

Before processing a full episode, start with one representative recording. Check the opening sentence, ending, captions, and crop. A useful short should make sense to someone who has never watched the original. Restore any sentence needed to explain a claim, and check that captions preserve names and technical terms.

Compare limits against your source recording, not just the length of the finished clip. A short export can still require analyzing a long upload. Also distinguish the ongoing free plan from any temporary upgrade offered during registration; the recurring plan’s restrictions determine whether the tool fits your routine.

What is the best free AI clip maker?

Vizard is the strongest starting point for most readers because its free plan includes AI clip generation and an editor. Expect watermarked 720p exports and limited monthly credits.

Which free AI clip maker has no watermark?

2short.ai advertises watermark-free exports on its free Starter plan, with limited analysis time. Clipchamp also supports watermark-free free exports and works well for AI-assisted visual highlight reels. 2short.ai features, Clipchamp export details.

Can AI turn a long YouTube video into Shorts for free?

Yes. For your own captioned YouTube videos, 2short.ai accepts a pasted link and generates short excerpts within its free allowance. Review each clip’s context and captions before publishing. Supported videos and workflow.
