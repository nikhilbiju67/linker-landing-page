6 free online tools to remove background from image files

By the Linkker team · 21 October 2026 · 6 min read

To remove an image background for free, upload your photo to Pixelcut, check the cutout, and download a transparent PNG. These six browser tools suit people making profile pictures, presentation graphics, product photos, or collages; some offer free HD downloads, while others restrict the free result to a small preview.

Choose by what you need to download, not just how the preview looks. A cutout that works as a tiny avatar may be too small for a poster, and free personal use does not always include permission to use the result commercially.

Quick picks

  1. Pixelcut: HD cutouts without an account
  2. Photopea: Manual control over troublesome cutouts
  3. Pixlr: Several straightforward images at once
  4. Photoroom: Product cutouts with simple replacement backgrounds
  5. remove.bg: Small personal images and draft layouts
  6. Slazzer: Personal previews and comparing automatic selections
  1. 1 P

    Pixelcut

    pixelcut.ai
    Free

    Remove a photo background and download an HD cutout without signing up.

    Pixelcut is a practical first stop because its dedicated tool explicitly offers free HD downloads without a watermark or account. Upload a JPG, PNG, or HEIC image, let it separate the subject, then use Refine to brush edges that need attention. You can export a transparent PNG or switch to a white background.

    That combination suits quick headshots, presentation graphics, and individual product images. The catch is the upload ceiling: images must fit its file-size and pixel-dimension limits. Check those before uploading a large camera original. Supported formats and free download details.

    Best for
    HD cutouts without an account
    The catch
    Uploads have file-size and pixel-dimension limits; difficult edges may need manual refinement.
    Visit pixelcut.ai ↗
  2. 2

    Photopea

    photopea.com
    Free with ads

    Cut out a subject with editable brush marks and export it as a transparent PNG.

    Photopea is useful when automatic removal keeps deleting part of your subject. Open your photo, choose Select → Magic Cut, then mark what to keep in green and what to remove in red. Its side-by-side preview lets you adjust the selection before committing.

    Choose this for objects with gaps, awkward outlines, or backgrounds that need closer attention. It takes more work than a single upload button, but you can control the boundary and keep editing afterward. Export through File → Export As → PNG to preserve transparency. Free Magic Cut instructions.

    Best for
    Manual control over troublesome cutouts
    The catch
    The editor shows ads, and Magic Cut requires brush work rather than a fully automatic workflow.
    Visit photopea.com ↗
  3. 3

    Pixlr

    pixlr.com
    Free plan

    Remove backgrounds from several images and save the results together.

    Pixlr’s dedicated background remover accepts multiple photos, automatically isolates their subjects, and offers a ZIP download for the results. It also lets you save with a transparent, black, or white background. That makes it useful for a small set of presentation images or matching cutouts for a collage.

    Its supported upload formats include JPEG, PNG, and BMP. The main tradeoff is control: this page emphasizes automatic processing rather than detailed masking. Inspect each result before saving the whole set, especially images containing fine hair or overlapping objects. Batch and export options.

    Best for
    Several straightforward images at once
    The catch
    The dedicated remover offers limited control over difficult selections; detailed cleanup needs an editor.
    Visit pixlr.com ↗

    Photoroom

    photoroom.com
    Free plan

    Isolate a product or person and choose a transparent or solid-color background.

    Photoroom fits the common job of turning a cluttered product photo into a simple cutout. Its web tool removes the background, then lets you keep transparency or choose white or another color. Download options include PNG, JPEG, and WebP; choose PNG when you need transparency.

    Use it for occasional product-photo preparation or a clean subject to place in another design. Background removal is available free, but high-resolution exports and additional AI editing features require an upgrade. Check the downloadable size before preparing an image for a large layout. Free tool and export details.

    Best for

    The catch
    High-resolution exports and additional AI editing tools are restricted.

    remove.bg

    Free plan

    Automatically make a small transparent cutout for personal use.

    remove.bg handles the basic upload-and-cut-out workflow with little setup. Its free website output is a low-resolution preview, which can suit a personal avatar, a small collage element, or a draft layout. It is less suitable when the subject needs to fill a large area.

    The limitation goes beyond image size: the official help page specifies personal use for the free service and directs commercial use to paid options. Choose it when both restrictions fit your project, rather than assuming every downloadable preview is ready for a business listing. Official free-use restrictions.

    Slazzer

    Create free low-resolution background-removal previews in your browser.

    Slazzer provides another automatic option when you want to compare how a different tool separates your subject. Its website offers ongoing free previews, so the core preview workflow does not depend on a trial timer. Upload one image at a time and inspect the outline before downloading.

    Choose it for personal graphics or evaluating a cutout at a small size. Its own plan page recommends the free service for personal use and evaluation; ongoing HD output and commercial use require paid access. How to remove an image background for free with Pixelcut

  1. Open the background remover. Use Pixelcut’s dedicated web tool on your phone or computer. Its page offers free HD downloads without sign-up or a watermark.
  2. Upload your original photo. Select a supported JPG, PNG, or HEIC file within the upload limits. Start with the clearest version you have; a blurry screenshot gives the tool less detail to separate.
  3. Inspect the automatic cutout. Zoom in on hair, fingers, straps, and spaces between objects. Look for missing parts of the subject as well as pieces of background left behind.
  4. Use Refine where needed. Brush the areas that need correction. If the outline remains awkward, try Photopea’s Magic Cut for more direct control.
  5. Download a transparent PNG. Keep the background transparent if you plan to place the subject over another image. Choose white only when you want white to be part of the finished picture. These options are described on Pixelcut’s tool page.
  6. Check the saved file in your destination. Place it in your slide, document, or design and inspect it at the size people will see. A clean-looking thumbnail can hide rough edges.

Quick answers

Which free online tool can remove an image background without signing up?

Pixelcut explicitly offers background removal and free HD downloads without an account. Its dedicated tool also states that the download has no watermark. Upload limits still apply. Pixelcut’s download details.

Why does my removed background turn white when I save it?

You may have saved as JPEG or selected a white background. JPEG cannot preserve transparency. Export as PNG and keep the background transparent; Photopea’s Magic Cut guide explains that distinction.

Can I use a free AI video generator from image files after making a cutout?

A cutout can be a starting asset, but the video tool must accept that file format, and it may flatten transparency or create a new background. Keep a copy of the original photo too.
