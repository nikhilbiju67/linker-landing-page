12 best free tools for project management, with the catch for each

By the Linkker team · 27 October 2026 · 8 min read

These are the best free tools for project management for freelancers, students, and small teams who need to assign work, track deadlines, and see what happens next.

Each qualifies because its ongoing free plan covers a useful project workflow without a trial timer, with the limits checked against official plan pages.

Quick picks

  1. Trello: Simple visual teamwork
  2. ClickUp: Teams wanting tasks and docs together
  3. Freedcamp: Groups that need room for more participants
  4. Teamwork.com: Small teams delivering client work
  5. Zoho Projects: Small projects with linked deadlines
  6. Asana: Solo projects and working pairs
  7. Todoist: Personal projects and recurring work
  8. Notion: Solo projects with substantial notes
  9. Jira: Small teams managing structured backlogs
  10. Airtable: Projects built around structured information
  11. Wrike: Larger groups with a modest active workload
  12. GitHub: Software teams already using GitHub
  1. 1

    Trello

    trello.com
    Free plan

    Move tasks across visual boards so everyone can see what needs doing next.

    Trello is the safest starting point for a small team with a straightforward workflow. Create lists such as To do, Doing, and Done, then give each task a card with an owner and due date. The free plan includes unlimited cards, so a busy board does not immediately force an upgrade.

    Its standout is how little explanation the board needs. Use it for a website launch, class assignment, or content schedule when visible progress matters more than detailed reporting. Official free-plan details.

    Best for
    Simple visual teamwork
    The catch
    Workspace-wide collaborator and board caps apply, including guests; attachments and monthly automation runs are limited.

How to choose between them

Start with team size and the way work moves. Trello is the easiest default for a small shared board. ClickUp offers more structure and documents, while Freedcamp is useful when a small collaborator allowance would exclude people. For solo work, Asana and Todoist offer clear task tracking; Notion suits projects with substantial notes and research.

Check the constraint that will actually stop your work. A client team may hit a project cap first. An operations group may hit an active-task cap. A research project may run out of attachment storage. Guest permissions also matter when clients should see only their own work.

Before moving everything, create one representative project with a real deadline, several tasks, and the people who need access. Confirm that the required views and permissions belong to the ongoing free plan. Quick answers

What is the best free project management tool for a small team?

Trello is the safest general starting point for a simple visual workflow. Choose ClickUp if you also need project documents, or Freedcamp if collaborator limits are the main obstacle.

Which project management tools are free forever?

All twelve offer an ongoing free plan for the core workflow described here. Advanced features may be restricted or offered through a separate trial, and free-plan terms can change.

Can I manage projects for free without a credit card?

Yes. Teamwork.com and Zoho Projects explicitly offer free signup without a credit card. Select the ongoing free plan and check the account's plan settings before relying on any advanced features.
