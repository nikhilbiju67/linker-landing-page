good questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Linkker free?

Yes. Linkker is free to download and use on iPhone, Android and Chrome. Linkker Pro is an optional subscription that unlocks unlimited saves and collections.

How do I save a link?

On your phone, tap Share in any app and choose Linkker. In Chrome, click the Linkker extension to save the current tab. Linkker can also offer to save a link you just copied.

How does the AI categorisation work?

When you save a link, Linkker looks at the page’s address, title and description and suggests a category like Recipes, Design or Read later. You can always move links or create your own collections.

What does “Not safe for work” do?

Links marked as protected or “Not safe for work” are hidden in your lists and search results, and can’t be opened, copied, shared or shown as a QR code until you unlock with your PIN, Face ID or fingerprint.

Do file transfers go through your servers?

No. After you pair your phone and computer with a QR code, links and files are sent directly between them over your own Wi-Fi network. Both devices need to be on the same network.

Will my links sync between devices?

Yes. Sign in with the same Google or Apple account on your phone and in the Chrome extension, and your links and collections stay in sync.

How do I cancel Linkker Pro?

Subscriptions bought through the App Store or Google Play are managed in your phone’s subscription settings. For subscriptions bought from the Chrome extension, contact support and we’ll help.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile and choose Delete account (on Android it’s under Profile → Settings). You can also request deletion without the app.
