Delete your Linkker account

This page explains how to delete your account for Linkker (the iPhone app, the Android app com.linkker.app and the Chrome extension), developed by Nikhil Biju, and what happens to your data.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

The quickest way. Deletion starts immediately and you’ll be signed out.

On Android

  1. Open the Linkker app and sign in
  2. Tap Profile
  3. Tap Settings
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm

On iPhone

  1. Open the Linkker app and sign in
  2. Tap Profile
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. If you have a Linkker Pro subscription through the App Store or Google Play, cancel it in your store’s subscription settings as well. Deleting your account doesn’t stop store billing.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

No longer have the app installed? Fill in this form. It opens your email app with a pre-written request to nikhilbiju6449@gmail.com. Please send it from the email address you use to sign in to Linkker so we can verify the account is yours. Nothing is sent until you press Send in your email app.

The Google or Apple email you sign in to Linkker with. If you used “Hide My Email”, enter the Apple relay address.

Prefer to write it yourself? Email nikhilbiju6449@gmail.com with the subject “Linkker account deletion request” and the email address on your account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile: name, email address, profile picture and sign-in identifiers
  • All saved links, notes, categories, collections and folders, including protected and “Not safe for work” links
  • Your settings and preferences
  • Paired transfer devices and their device records (name, local network address, online status)
  • Your subscription link to your account in our systems

Files you sent with phone-to-computer transfer were never stored on our servers. Copies remain only on your own devices.

Timeline

  • In-app deletion: your account is closed immediately and your data is erased from our live systems within 30 days.
  • Email requests: we reply within 3 business days, verify that the request came from the account’s email address, and complete deletion within 30 days of verification.
  • Backups: any remaining copies are removed within 90 days.
