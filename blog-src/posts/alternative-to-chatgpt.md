## Quick answers

### What is the best free alternative to ChatGPT?

Choose by the job. Claude is a useful starting point for revising writing, Perplexity for finding sources, and Duck.ai for chatting without an account. These are task-based recommendations, not a claim that one assistant wins every comparison.

### Which websites like ChatGPT work without signing up?

Duck.ai explicitly offers free access without an account. Microsoft also describes basic Copilot access without sign-in, while reserving features such as history and longer conversations for signed-in users. Both still have access restrictions.

### What is a free alternative to Claude AI?

Gemini and Mistral Vibe cover drafting, explanations, and general conversation. Perplexity fits better when your main need is web research with citations. Your existing Claude chats and preferences will not automatically carry into another assistant.

### Can free tools replace a paid AI subscription?

They can cover occasional writing, explanations, and basic research. The harder replacement is sustained work with large uploads, advanced models, or repeated research tasks. Check the allowance for your actual workflow, and remember that switching services means supplying relevant context again.
