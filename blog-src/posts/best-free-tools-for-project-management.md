## How to choose between them

Start with team size and the way work moves. Trello is the easiest default for a small shared board. ClickUp offers more structure and documents, while Freedcamp is useful when a small collaborator allowance would exclude people. For solo work, Asana and Todoist offer clear task tracking; Notion suits projects with substantial notes and research.

Check the constraint that will actually stop your work. A client team may hit a project cap first. An operations group may hit an active-task cap. A research project may run out of attachment storage. Guest permissions also matter when clients should see only their own work.

Before moving everything, create one representative project with a real deadline, several tasks, and the people who need access. Confirm that the required views and permissions belong to the ongoing free plan. ## Quick answers

**What is the best free project management tool for a small team?**

Trello is the safest general starting point for a simple visual workflow. Choose ClickUp if you also need project documents, or Freedcamp if collaborator limits are the main obstacle.

**Which project management tools are free forever?**

All twelve offer an ongoing free plan for the core workflow described here. Advanced features may be restricted or offered through a separate trial, and free-plan terms can change.

**Can I manage projects for free without a credit card?**

Yes. Teamwork.com and Zoho Projects explicitly offer free signup without a credit card. Select the ongoing free plan and check the account's plan settings before relying on any advanced features.
