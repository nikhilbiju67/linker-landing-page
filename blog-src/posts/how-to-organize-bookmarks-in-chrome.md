To organize bookmarks in Chrome, open Bookmark Manager, create a few folders, and drag your saved pages into them. That’s enough for most people who want to find yesterday’s link without scrolling through years of saves. [Google’s Chrome bookmark guide](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/188842)

Start with the links you use every week. Once those are easy to reach, pick a tool for whatever still slows you down, such as duplicate bookmarks, a backlog of articles, or links that keep getting stranded on your phone.

## A ten-minute bookmark routine

1. **Back up what you have.** In Chrome Bookmark Manager, open the three-dot menu and select **Export bookmarks**. Keep the HTML file before you delete duplicates or rearrange a large collection. [Chrome’s export instructions](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/96816)
2. **Put frequent links within reach.** Keep the pages you open every week on the bookmarks bar. Give the rest a few folders named for things you do, such as `Pay bills`, `Plan trips`, or `Work reference`. If you share links outside Chrome, see these [ways to save links in a folder](/blog/how-to-save-links-in-a-folder).
3. **Clear a small batch once a week.** Rename vague titles, delete links you’re done with, and file the ones you’ll use again. If most of the pile is unread articles, give them a queue in a [read-it-later app](/blog/read-it-later-apps).

## Quick answers

### How do I organize bookmarks into folders in Chrome?

Open **Bookmarks and lists → Bookmark Manager** from Chrome’s three-dot menu. Create a folder from the manager’s three-dot menu, then drag existing bookmarks onto it in the sidebar. To file a new bookmark, click the star in the address bar and choose the folder when you save it. [Google’s folder instructions](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/188842)

### How do I organize the bookmarks bar in Chrome?

Go to **Bookmarks and lists → Show bookmarks bar**. Drag bookmarks left or right to reorder them. Right-click the bar and choose **Add folder** to group related links. Shorten long bookmark names so more of them fit. [Google’s bookmarks bar instructions](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/188842)

### How do I organize bookmarks on a Mac?

In Chrome, press **Command + Option + B** to open Bookmark Manager. **Command + Shift + B** shows or hides the bookmarks bar. In Safari, open **Bookmarks** in the sidebar. Use its **More** menu to create a folder or choose a sort order. To drag items into your own order, turn off **Show Folders on Top** first. [Chrome keyboard shortcuts](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/157179) · [Apple’s Safari bookmark guide](https://support.apple.com/en-mide/guide/safari/ibrw462936b2/mac)

### How do I organize bookmarks in Edge?

Edge calls them **Favorites**. Open **Settings and more → Favorites**, then choose **More options → Open favorites page**. From there you can create folders and move links, or right-click and choose **Sort by name** for alphabetical order. [Microsoft’s Favorites guide](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/edge/organize-favorites-in-microsoft-edge)
