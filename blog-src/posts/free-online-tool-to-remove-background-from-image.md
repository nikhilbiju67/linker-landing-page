## How to remove an image background for free with Pixelcut

1. **Open the background remover.** Use Pixelcut’s dedicated web tool on your phone or computer. Its page offers free HD downloads without sign-up or a watermark.
2. **Upload your original photo.** Select a supported JPG, PNG, or HEIC file within the upload limits. Start with the clearest version you have; a blurry screenshot gives the tool less detail to separate.
3. **Inspect the automatic cutout.** Zoom in on hair, fingers, straps, and spaces between objects. Look for missing parts of the subject as well as pieces of background left behind.
4. **Use Refine where needed.** Brush the areas that need correction. If the outline remains awkward, try Photopea’s Magic Cut for more direct control.
5. **Download a transparent PNG.** Keep the background transparent if you plan to place the subject over another image. Choose white only when you want white to be part of the finished picture. These options are described on [Pixelcut’s tool page](https://www.pixelcut.ai/background-remover).
6. **Check the saved file in your destination.** Place it in your slide, document, or design and inspect it at the size people will see. A clean-looking thumbnail can hide rough edges.

**Why does my removed background turn white when I save it?**

You may have saved as JPEG or selected a white background. JPEG cannot preserve transparency. Export as PNG and keep the background transparent; Photopea’s [Magic Cut guide](https://www.photopea.com/tuts/magic-cut-remove-image-background-online/) explains that distinction.
