**Quick answers**

**What are some free tools for students that don't require signing up?**

ZoteroBib handles quick bibliographies, PhET runs interactive simulations, and draw.io makes diagrams without registration. Desmos also lets you begin graphing before signing in. Save or export anything you need to keep.

**What are the best AI tools for students?**

Choose by task: Goblin Tools helps break assignments into steps, while ResearchRabbit helps discover related papers. Both offer useful free functionality. Neither removes the need to check suggestions and read original sources.

**Which are useful websites for kids and teachers?**

PhET is a good starting point for exploring science and math together. GeoGebra suits interactive math demonstrations and constructions. Pick an activity that matches the learner's level and give them one specific thing to investigate.
