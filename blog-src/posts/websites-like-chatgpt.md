## Quick answers

### What is the best free alternative to ChatGPT?

Start with Google Gemini if you want one chat for everyday writing, brainstorming, and questions about files. Pick Claude if most of your time goes into revising prose, or Perplexity if you usually need links you can open and verify. Try the same real task in two tools and see which gives you a better first answer and handles your follow-up with fewer corrections.

### Is there an alternative to ChatGPT without signing up?

Duck.ai lets you start a free chat and choose a model without creating an account. It suits quick tasks like shortening an email or explaining a spreadsheet formula. The daily cap matters more if you plan to use it for repeated follow-ups. [Duck.ai access details](https://duckduckgo.com/duckduckgo-help-pages/duckai/approach-to-ai)

### Which free ChatGPT alternative is best for research papers?

Use Perplexity to find sources on the open web and Gemini Notebook to ask questions about PDFs you already have. For a literature review, use [free websites for finding research papers](/blog/best-websites-for-research-papers-free) to track down the original publications, then check the paper itself before citing a finding.
