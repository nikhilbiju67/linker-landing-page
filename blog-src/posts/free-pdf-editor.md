## How to choose a free PDF editor

Start with the change you need to make. For an existing typo, choose PDFgear, Sejda, or LibreOffice Draw. For a date, signature, or answer in a blank space, SimplePDF, Preview, or your browser may be enough. For handwritten explanations, Xournal++ is a better fit.

A scanned page is a picture of text. Adding an annotation works, but rewriting its words usually requires optical character recognition, or OCR, first. An editor’s ability to add text does not establish that it can edit a scan.

For sensitive paperwork, choose an offline app or an editor that explicitly processes documents locally. Work on a copy, reopen the saved PDF, and check that text, signatures, and form answers appear correctly. Covering text with a white rectangle is not secure redaction.

If the document needs substantial rewriting, [converting PDF to Word](/blog/how-to-convert-pdf-to-word) may be easier than moving individual text objects around. If the finished file exceeds an upload allowance, these [free PDF compression tools](/blog/how-to-compress-pdf-file-size) address that separate job.

## Quick answers

**Which free PDF filler also works as a free PDF reader?**

Preview, Firefox, and Edge can read PDFs and fill supported forms. SimplePDF is useful when you need to place answers on a document that has no fillable fields.

**Is a free PDF merger the same as a free PDF combiner?**

Yes. Both combine separate PDFs into one file. PDF24 and iLovePDF offer this job; arrange the files in the intended order before saving the combined document.

**Can I get a free PDF maker and free PDF converter?**

Yes. LibreOffice creates documents and exports PDFs, while PDF24 provides browser tools for creating and converting files. Conversion can change formatting, so check the result before sharing it.
