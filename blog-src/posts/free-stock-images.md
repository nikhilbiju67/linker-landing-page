## How to choose between these free stock images websites

For a routine blog post or presentation, start with Pexels, then try Unsplash. Move to Pixabay when an illustration or vector would explain the idea better than a photograph. Burst suits business themes, while Kaboompics helps when several images need to look like they belong together.

For a named place, historical subject, or scientific topic, use Wikimedia Commons and budget a little time for attribution. ISO Republic and NegativeSpace are useful extra sources when your first searches stall. Save Gratisography for projects that can carry its playful style.

Before downloading, check three things: the image’s license, whether its subjects fit your intended use, and whether the composition survives your crop. A beautiful horizontal photo may become useless in a narrow mobile card. Keep the source page and license details with the finished asset, especially when a client or colleague will publish it later.

For websites, resize the image to the dimensions you actually need before uploading. If the resulting file is still too heavy, these [free tools to reduce image size](/blog/free-online-tool-to-reduce-image-size) cover the next step.

## Quick answers

**Where can I get free stock images no watermark?**

Pexels and the free Unsplash library are good starting points. Download the actual image rather than saving a preview, and check its license before publishing. No watermark does not mean there are no usage restrictions.

**Which free stock video websites offer free stock video download?**

Pexels, Pixabay, and ISO Republic offer free stock videos alongside images. Choose the video category before searching, and use the clip’s own download page to check the applicable terms.

**Are free stock photos free for commercial use?**

Many are, including photos covered by the Pexels and free Unsplash licenses. Commercial permission still comes with conditions: recognizable people, logos, and artwork may need additional clearance. Wikimedia Commons requires a file-by-file license check, and Kaboompics marks some images Editorial Use Only.
