These seven tools show how to convert PDF to Word for free when you need to edit a document in your browser: upload the PDF, start the conversion, then download the Word file. Start with PDF24 for ordinary documents; if the pages are scans or photos, use an option with optical character recognition, or OCR.

Before uploading, try selecting a sentence in your PDF. Selectable text usually makes conversion simpler; a page that behaves like one large picture needs OCR. Keep the original so you can compare formatting afterward, and use these [free alternatives to Microsoft Word](/blog/free-alternative-to-microsoft-word) if you need somewhere to edit the result.

## How to convert a PDF to a Word document with PDF24

1. Open [PDF24’s PDF to Word tool](https://tools.pdf24.org/en/pdf-to-word) and click **Choose files**.
2. Select the PDF from your phone or computer. If its text cannot be selected because the pages are scans, follow the tool’s OCR link first, then return to conversion.
3. Click **Convert** and wait for processing to finish.
4. Download the Word file and open it in your preferred document editor.
5. Compare it with the original before making changes. Check headings, page breaks, tables, and any text next to images. Save an untouched copy of the converted file before editing.

A PDF fixes content in position, while Word needs editable paragraphs and objects. Conversion can change spacing or split a paragraph into separate blocks. If the result needs extensive cleanup, try a second converter before rebuilding the layout by hand.

### Quick answers

**What is a free PDF to Word converter online that needs no sign-up?**

PDF24 requires no registration and advertises no usage limits. PDFgear is another free option, including an OCR mode for scanned documents, with file size and single-file restrictions. Neither requires starting a timed trial for the core conversion. [PDF24](https://tools.pdf24.org/en/pdf-to-word), [PDFgear](https://www.pdfgear.com/pdf-to-word/).

**How to convert JPG to PDF, or another image to PDF?**

Use iLovePDF’s separate **JPG to PDF** tool. Upload your pictures, arrange their order, choose the page settings, and convert. This places images inside a PDF; it does not recognize their text or make that text editable. [JPG to PDF tool](https://www.ilovepdf.com/jpg_to_pdf).

**How to convert PDF to Excel instead of Word?**

Use Sejda’s separate **PDF to Excel** converter to extract table data into a spreadsheet. Check column alignment, dates, totals, and decimal separators afterward. Converting a table through Word first adds an unnecessary step. [PDF to Excel tool](https://www.sejda.com/pdf-to-excel).
