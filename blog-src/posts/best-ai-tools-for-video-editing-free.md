## How to choose between them

Start with the part of editing that costs you the most time. Clipchamp covers the broadest everyday workflow: captions, voiceovers, pause removal, and a clean full-HD export. Canva and Adobe Express make more sense when the finished video needs graphics and readable text. Descript suits recordings where you mainly want to cut and rearrange speech.

For regular work without cloud transcription quotas, consider Shotcut or Kdenlive. Their tradeoff is setup and processing time on your computer. VEED and Kapwing are easier to justify for occasional short clips when their watermarks and allowances fit the job.

Before committing to any editor, export a short sample with the exact assets and features you intend to use. Check the resulting resolution, watermark, caption placement, and audio. That catches restrictions before you spend an afternoon on the full project. Always review generated captions and automatic cuts yourself.

## Quick answers

**What is a good free AI video maker?**

For assembling your own clips with AI assistance, Clipchamp is a practical first choice. A free AI video maker that edits existing footage is different from a generator that creates new scenes from prompts; the free editing features listed here do not imply unlimited video generation.

**What free video editor app works on Mac?**

Shotcut runs on Mac and includes AI subtitle generation after downloading a speech model. It suits people who want an installed editor. Canva also offers a free mobile video editing app, while Clipchamp works on Mac through a supported browser.

**How to compress video?**

In an editor such as Shotcut, export a copy at a lower resolution or lower video bitrate. Trim unused footage first, then compare the result with your original. Keep the original file: compression can soften detail, and exporting repeatedly can reduce quality further.
