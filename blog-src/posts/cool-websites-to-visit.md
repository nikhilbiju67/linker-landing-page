**Quick answers**

**What are some cool websites for fun?**

Start with The Password Game for a challenge, Patatap for instant sound and animation, or MapCrunch for a random virtual walk.

**Which cool websites for kids are worth opening together?**

Song Maker is a good starting point for making music. AutoDraw offers an approachable drawing activity, while NASA's Eyes gives older children space topics to explore with an adult.

**Where can I find cool websites with animation?**

Zoomquilt offers continuous illustrated motion, Patatap responds to your input, and earth animates weather patterns. Choose Patatap for interaction or earth when you want the movement to explain something.
